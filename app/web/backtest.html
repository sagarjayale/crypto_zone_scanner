Backtest

Pick one scan date. From this date to today runs the rules on every closed candle from that date until now, exactly as live: setups found, limit orders placed, filled when price touches them, then stopped, at target, trailed or still open (trailing as set in Settings). This date only applies the rules with the candles up to that date, then follows the entry type its trade score takes through the candles after it. Results are in R, where 1 R is the distance from entry to stop loss, after Binance fees (maker for a limit entry or a target, taker for a market entry or a stop) and the funding paid or received at each funding time the trade was open over. Nothing is downloaded: it runs on the stored candles. Dates are UTC days.
