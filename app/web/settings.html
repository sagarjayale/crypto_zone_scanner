Settings

Binance and live prices

Market data comes from Binance USDT-M futures. It is public: no API key is needed, and the app never places an order.

Coin list

USDT perpetuals the scanner downloads and scans. On the very first start it is filled with the top coins by 24 hour volume. A new coin is downloaded and scanned in the background.

Zone rules

Zone marking

How a zone is found, tested, used up and scored, and how much history is searched. The Backtest page reads these settings at once, so you can check the same scan date under each. The dashboard follows after the rebuild.

Analysis modes

Each mode reads an HTF for location and curve, an ITF for trend, and an LTF for the execution zone. The scanner runs the ticked ones.

Trade side rule

How the curve and the ITF trend decide between long, short and no trade, and when the ITF counts as up or down. Saving rescans every coin so the dashboard follows the rule you chose.

Trade score and entry type

The trade score is freshness (fresh 3, tested once 1.5, tested twice 0) plus strength (a gap or two exciting candles 2, one exciting candle 1) plus time at base (1 to 3 candles 2, 4 to 5 candles 1, more 0), plus 1 when the 20 or 50 EMA runs through the zone. A score of 7 or more takes entry type 1, a limit at the proximal line. A score of 5 to 6.5 takes entry type 2 or 3. Below 5 there is no trade. The stop loss is the distal line for all three.

These only filter which zones are traded (dashboard, alerts, paper, backtest and checks). How zones are found and scored does not change, and at 0 everything works as before.

Costs

Binance USDT-M futures fees for a regular account. Entry type 1 and targets are limit orders and pay the maker fee; entry types 2 and 3, stop losses and manual closes take the price that is there and pay the taker fee. Funding is paid or received at each funding time a trade is open over, from the stored funding history. The backtest, the scan-date check and paper trading use these.

History kept

Days of candles downloaded the first time and kept afterwards, for the short timeframes. 2 hours and above are kept from the coin's listing date. These reach well past what the zone rules search; raise them only for longer backtests (more history makes the first download and the database bigger). After that, only the candles that closed since the last one stored are downloaded.

Time zone

Trade management: trailing stop loss

Off by default: every trade keeps its first stop and its target, as before. When on, once price has gone the chosen number of R in the trade's favour the stop moves to break-even (entry plus fees), then follows the trade: step moves it up 0.5 R at a time (at +2 R the stop is at +1 R), swing puts it under each new swing low (above each swing high for a short), ATR keeps it a number of ATRs behind the best price, lock moves it in steps of a % of the distance to the target (entry 100, target 200, 10 %: at 112 the stop goes to 110, at 125 to 120) and can move the target forward when price reaches it. It only ever tightens and moves when a candle of the chosen timeframe closes. Paper trading uses these settings for new positions; a backtest run uses them unless it names its own.
