Paper trading

Practice with the scanner's own plans. A trade score of 7 or more is an entry type 1 order (a limit at the proximal line); a score of 5 to 6.5 is an entry type 2 or 3 order, which waits for its candles. Fills are simulated against live Binance mark prices; no order is ever sent to Binance. Each order risks your chosen share of the balance at its stop loss, rounded to the coin's step size, at the leverage you set. Profit and loss is after fees (maker for a limit entry or target, taker for a market entry, stop or manual close) and funding.
