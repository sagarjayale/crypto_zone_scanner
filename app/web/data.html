Stored candles

Candles are downloaded from Binance USDT-M futures (public data, no key) and kept here, in UTC. Download and scan fetches only the candles that closed since the last one stored, then scans them. It runs in the background: each coin can be used on the other pages as soon as it is ready, while the rest carry on. The schedule does this by itself every minute, around the clock.
